# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA & Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, testing methodologies, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure deliverables meet defined standards before release and help the team design testable solutions from the start.

### Responsibilities
- Define testing strategy and QA approach during planning
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria checklists
- Execute manual QA and coordinate automated testing efforts
- Identify quality gaps and regressions early
- Participate in release readiness reviews
- Document known issues and workarounds

### Goals
- Catch defects early and reduce production incidents
- Enable fast, confident releases with high quality
- Build testability into product design

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement
- Test case reviews and acceptance criteria walkthroughs
- Pre-release smoke test coordination
- Bug triage and defect tracking

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors and key stakeholders provide business context, approve investments, and ensure projects deliver strategic value. They are decision-makers who balance priorities and remove blockers at the executive level.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and success metrics
- Approve project initiation and budget/resources
- Review and approve major milestones and releases
- Escalate business-impacting risks and blockers
- Communicate project outcomes to leadership and customers
- Support team by removing organizational obstacles

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain strategic alignment and priority clarity
- Support team success by providing air cover and resources

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval gates
- Monthly or milestone-based stakeholder updates
- Executive escalations and decision requests
- Post-release outcomes and ROI reviews

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects define technical direction, ensure scalability and maintainability, and guide design decisions. They balance technical excellence with delivery timelines.

### Responsibilities
- Design technical solutions and architecture
- Review technical approach for scalability and risk
- Mentor team on technical best practices
- Identify technical dependencies and integration points
- Support capacity planning and effort estimation
- Make trade-off decisions on technical debt vs. speed

### Goals
- Build robust, maintainable, and scalable systems
- Reduce technical risk and unplanned rework
- Enable team learning and growth

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Planning and estimation sessions
- Code review and mentorship
- Risk identification and mitigation planning

---

## Security Officer / CISO Representative

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure projects meet security standards, compliance requirements, and organizational risk policies. They partner with teams to build security into the development lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and compliance standards
- Review designs and implementations for security risks
- Coordinate security scanning and vulnerability remediation
- Approve security exceptions and mitigation plans
- Respond to and investigate security incidents
- Maintain incident response and breach notification procedures

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Ensure compliance with regulations and policies
- Build a security-conscious culture

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements gathering during planning
- Security architecture and code reviews
- Vulnerability reporting and remediation tracking
- Incident response activation and post-mortems
- Security incident escalation and communication
